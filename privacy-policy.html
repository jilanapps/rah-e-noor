🕌 Privacy Policy — Rah-e-Noor

Last updated: October 11, 2026

1. No Account, No Tracking

Rah-e-Noor does not require you to create an account. We do not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools.

2. Data Stored On Your Device Only

Your preferences (dark mode, city, bookmarks, tasbeeh counts, checklist progress) are saved only on your own phone using your browser/app local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

3. Location Permission

The app may request your location only to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your area. Your location is used on-device and is never transmitted, stored, or shared. You may deny location access and select your city manually instead.

4. Internet Usage

The app fetches public religious content (Quran text, translations, prayer times) from free public APIs. No personal data is included in these requests.

5. Children's Privacy

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.

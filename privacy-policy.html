🕌 Privacy Policy — Rah-e-Noor

Last updated: October 14, 2026

1. No Account, No Tracking

Rah-e-Noor does not require you to create an account. We do not collect, store, or share your personal information. We do not use analytics, advertising SDKs, or tracking tools. The app shows no ads.

2. Data Stored On Your Device Only

Your preferences (dark mode, city, bookmarks, tasbeeh counts, checklist progress, favourites) are saved only on your own phone using the app's local storage. This data never leaves your device and is never sent to us or any third party.

3. Location Permission

The app may request your location only to calculate accurate prayer times and Qibla direction for your area. Your location is used on-device and is never transmitted, stored, or shared. You may deny location access and select your city manually instead.

4. Internet Usage

The app fetches public religious content (Quran text, translations, prayer times) from free public sources. No personal data is included in these requests. When the app loads online content, your device connects to our hosting (GitHub Pages) the same way any website loads; we do not see or store your identity from that connection.

5. Downloaded Images & Sharing

When you download a Daily Islamic Image, it is saved to your own Gallery (Pictures / Rah-e-Noor folder) on your device. When you share something, the app opens your phone's own share menu — the content goes only to the app you choose, and we never see or store what you share.

6. Children's Privacy

The app is safe for all ages and collects no data from anyone, including children.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this same address.
